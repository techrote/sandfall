  and refused registration operations now leave clock/candidate state intact.

content hashes plus tick work with 1/4 workers for 24 ticks. They establish reader
neutrality in those fixtures, not automatic producer completeness. The
[24-route producer audit](issue-12-2026-09-19/producer-hook-review.md) identifies
mask ABA, event acceptance, worker-local activity/deadlines, policy changes,
region/failure visibility and custom geometry requirements. Render dirty consumption
and activity flags alone are insufficient. No speculative hook port was made.
