Explicitly enumerate **preserved** chemistry (Water+Salt, steam/ice products, cooling/extinguishing, density/body/temperature/transport precedence) and **deliberately different** Water-only FreeMass attributes (partial fills, coherent spray, film adhesion, if intentionally disabled). Do not merely redirect RuleKernel::Water into RuleKernel::YieldingLiquid. Require material×family hook unit tests/negative controls in #200/#212.
2. **Canonical policy hashing/wire validation.** The proposed policy_hash formula omits the separately listed capability_matrix_digest; include it and a canonical normalized assignment/variant/parameter schema. Define treatment of per-material parameters versus family-wide parameters and include policy components that actually affect execution (Water/transport/interaction identity in the complete run receipt). Reject duplicate/missing/unsorted IDs, reserved/nonliquid IDs, invalid signed ints, oversized counts, unknown mandatory parameter blobs, uncompiled platforms and stale digests *before* World replacement. Read back exact applied effective family/variant and any semantic normalization, not just requested selectors.
3. **State/quantity rule boundaries.** Specify per-family quantity transfer and foreign neighbor density/swap and collision semantics, pressure boundary invalidation and exact reset/wake behavior; cross-family conversion must carry source/sink ledgers, not assert whole-cell mass conservation from a partial-water source. Reconstruct a new *tick-zero scenario from retained setup* where that is all the caller can supply; do not call a CYSD1 level reconstruction an exact live-state resume. A failed/unsupported import keeps the live session.
5. **Matrix provenance.** tools/lcb/derive_capability_matrix.py parses checked-in descriptors/rules, **but PROPOSED/VARIANTS/KERNEL_SPECIALIZATION are reviewed hand-authored design policy**. Name these as independent source facts plus proposed choices, not mechanically derived executable solver coverage. No need to implement all four solvers to accept their *design* gate, but the corrected specification must be internally coherent and explicitly sourced.
