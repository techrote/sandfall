### S2A evidence means this—and no more

| Registered comparison | Observed median B/A | Qualification and decision |
|---|---:|---|
| A01: current source C64 E8 → C64 word E8 | 1.00160 | Near parity, not proof all access paths are identical in cost |
| A02: C64 word → padded64 | 1.02546 | Small overhead in registered active-512, four-worker anchor |
| A03/A04: C64 word → **packed C72/C80 E8** | **1.49605 / 1.51165** | Large **~50–51% tick overhead** for these packed representations *in the tested anchor*; significant reason not to migrate merely to obtain 8/16 more bits |
| A05/A06: C64 word → **aligned C72/C80 E8** | 1.01991 / 1.01797 | ~2% observed median; near-parity is **not** an occupancy/memory win or cross-platform guarantee |
| A07/A08: C64 word → **eager split C72/C80 E8** | 1.00001 / 1.00194 | Near parity in this anchor, not evidence that eager tails are sparse/optional or cheap to reclaim |
| A09–A16: within-arm E8 → E16, quiet-2048 one worker | medians **0.09202–0.72394** | Invalid as a **long-run E16 winner**: E8 natural clear fell at tick 256; E16 **never cleared** in this 120+256 horizon. Need separately admitted clear-latency/amortization/maintenance study |

The seven pair ranges overlap near 1.00 for several aligned/split cases, and the campaign is from one GitHub-hosted runner. S2A confirms a specific cost pattern **without extrapolating all platforms or all workloads**. The implementation alternatives (word-aligned, split storage, genuinely optional SoA, solver gas table) remain **architecture options**, not benchmark-selected production winners. Dense aligned padding may use a 16-byte stride; split eager 1B/2B tails logically use extra 1/2 bytes per cell but require explicit allocate/swap/erase semantics. An optional sidecar is **not** the same thing as S2A's eager split tail, and its peak occupancy/allocator/indexing costs remain unmeasured.

The [S2A observed process RSS table in PR #211](https://github.com/techrote/cybersand/pull/211) reports descriptive fresh-process peaks rather than fixed-memory equivalence, representative real-world allocations or per-cell active storage. C64's 8 bytes/cell gives 8 MiB for one fully resident 1024² cell payload and 32 MiB for 2048² before chunk/scheduler/temperature/gas/actors/rendering overhead. Packed72/80 theoretical cell payloads are 9/10 bytes; naturally aligned extended structs may stride 16; optional sidecar density and full-world peak RSS require independent measurement. No 72/80 capacity choice automatically satisfies gas pocket IDs, topology and velocities.

**Pending outside this Gate B design pass:** S2B full-grid work; natural E16 clear-latency/long-run amortization and epoch rebase; E24/E32 and 128-bit candidates if later registered; per-family actual state precision/capacity, heterogeneous mixed-family memory and race/lifecycle tests; platform-specific Windows/Wasm/Godot costs; fixed-memory method; GEN-006 streaming/save/continuation; any migration/rollback gate. #204 remains OPEN and #208/#211 remain draft.

### Adopted storage contract (unchanged from #216)

Keep **C64-E8 production `Cell` (64-bit, eight-byte aligned)** and the exact Current controls. Prepare only **bounded, optional and versioned solver-owned cell/chunk/World state** when a chosen material family requires it. Use a **separately World-owned authoritative gas-compartment table and boundary-to-region index**, not a gas amount masquerading as pocket identity; support Empty-only regions, merge/split/generation/invalidation, and deterministic reclamation. Allocate/validate before worker ticks and refuse unsupported capacities without changing the active session. Do not assign numerical bit widths by analogy with the archived 16 spare prototype bits.

The existing [Gate B authoritative-state field/operation matrix](2026-10-23-lcb000-gate-b-state-handoff.md) remains normative: material/catalogue/WaterPolicy, state_a/state_b, fractional and whole quantity, overfill/directional flow, inertial signed momentum plus subcell remainders, Water coherence, chemistry/source/sink, gas amount/topology/identity/generation, scheduler, mutation and external body/depth owners. Movement, partial transfer, swap, paint, erase, reaction, split/merge, rest/wake and reset must preserve authoritative ownership or explicitly account conversion/dissipation; a failed rebuild leaves the old World intact. A **new versioned full physical-state hash** must include every future-affecting cell, sidecar, gas and history field (including Empty-only gas). Preserve historical hashes unchanged; save/readback must reject unknown state rather than falsely treat raw C64 or CYSD1 as exact live continuation. Derived caches may be excluded only after a deterministic reconstruction proof. GEN-006 needs a separate explicit continuation/recipe/identity boundary.
